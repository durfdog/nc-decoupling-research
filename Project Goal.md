
- The goal of this project is to evaluate the extent to which the decoupling of economic growth from consumption based environmental footprint has occurred in North Carolina between 1997 and 2022. 

# Hypothesis

- I hypothesize that insufficient decoupling will have had occurred in the past to justify, with a high confidence, a green growth approach to environmentalism. 

# Why

- The purpose of this project is to inform legislators and the people about the historical effectiveness of green growth at a more local level. 
- My hope is that the methodology developed in this report can be extended to the analysis of other states or sub-national regions in the U.S. and elsewhere to help provide a more complete analysis of global decoupling. 

# Key points of interest and questions

- This report must differentiate between the decoupling of resource use (fossil fuels, water, biomass, minerals, etc.) and of impacts (GHG emissions, biodiversity loss, water pollution, non-GHG air pollution, agricultural runoff, etc.)
- This report must also differentiate between production and consumption based resource use and impact. The major focus will be consumption based to account for externalization of environmental pressure. 
- Decoupling, if found, must be analyzed as a temporal phenomenon, meaning it's stability and possibility of re-coupling must be taken into account. 
- Using the aforementioned consumption based footprints we can analyze the extent to which North Carolina's ecological footprint is overshooting it's fair, equitable bounds, both ecologically and socially.
